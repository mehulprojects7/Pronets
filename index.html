About ProNets

Founded in 2015, ProNets Basketball Academy has been steadily emerging as a strong platform for young players, focusing on skill development, confidence building, discipline, and teamwork.

Led by head coach and owner Ashutosh V Sangle, a national-level and NIS-certified coach, the academy operates across multiple schools and societies in Powai, Thane, and Ghatkopar.

With a dedicated coaching team and regular tournaments – including the signature intra-academy competition – every player, whether beginner or intermediate, gets equal opportunities to play, learn, and grow.
